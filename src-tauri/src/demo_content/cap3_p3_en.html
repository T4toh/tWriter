They left the tower in the rain. It wasn't a storm: it was a thin, steady rain, the kind that doesn't seem to hurry but soaks you anyway. Liriel looked at the fragment inside the traveler's bag and said nothing for a long while.

—It spoke —she said at last, without asking.

—Yes.

—And?

—It said I have to carry it whole again.

Liriel nodded slowly, as if the news confirmed something she had suspected before meeting him. They walked in silence for a good while, listening to the sound of water on the leaves. The slope went down in zigzags and the road, worn, seemed to have been walked hundreds of times and forgotten just the same.

—There are three more fragments —Liriel said eventually—. And not all of them are in quiet places.

—Do you know where?

—I know two. The third one, only the name.

—Which one?

—The Pact of Ash.

The traveler did not ask what that meant. He was going to find out one way or another.
