The last road climbed a slope that no longer resembled any other place. The stone changed color from one step to the next, grey to violet, violet to a raw white that hurt to look at straight on. The air was thinner and thinking moved at a slower speed.

Liriel stopped halfway up, on a ledge above the drop. The traveler looked at her without understanding.

—This far —she said—. The last stretch is only yours.

—Why?

—Because I already did it once. And it can't be done twice.

The traveler nodded, though he did not understand. He took the two fragments out of the bag and looked at them under the raw light of the sky. They fit almost on their own. The last one was missing, and the last one was up there, somewhere beyond the ridge in front of him.

—If I don't come back… —he began.

—You come back —Liriel cut him off—. Or you don't. Both are possible. Don't ask anyone to decide that for you.
