The village had been burned less than a month ago. The smell was still in the earth, beneath the fresher smell of wet leaves. The roofs were only black beams against the sky and the doors, where any remained, did not fit their frames.

An older woman was sitting in what had been the entrance of a house. Her hands lay still on her knees and she watched the road with the patience of someone who no longer waits for anyone in particular.

—There isn't much left —she said, without raising her eyes—. If you came to loot, you came too late.

—We didn't come to loot.

—Everyone says that at first.

The traveler crouched in front of her, slowly, so they were at the same height.

—We're looking for the Pact of Ash.

The woman's face did not move, but something changed in her breathing. A short pause. Enough.

—So you did come to loot —she said—. You just don't know it yet.
