He felt someone standing behind him before he turned. It wasn't Liriel. It was a translucent figure, dressed in light armor, feet not touching the floor. The armor, with its scars and dents, told a whole life without needing words.

—You picked it up —the spirit said, without moving its mouth—. Good. That's already something.

The traveler tightened his grip on the fragment. The voice did not come from outside; it came from inside the chest, as if the conversation were happening elsewhere and he heard it as a rebound.

—It's you. —The spirit looked at him without curiosity, the way one reviews an old paper—. It's you, after all.

—Me, what?

—The one who has to carry it whole again. —It made a gesture with the transparent hand—. Don't get excited. It isn't an honor. It is a job, and a heavy one.

The traveler felt something in the fragment settle into the weight of his hand, as if it were finally in the place it had always meant to be.
