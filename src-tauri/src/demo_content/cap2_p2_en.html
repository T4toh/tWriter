The creatures appeared between the trees without a single warning sound. There were three of them, tall as men but thinner, with arms too long and eyes too still. The traveler understood, without having to think it through, that they were not animals.

—Don't draw the knife —the woman said quietly—. It won't help here.

—What will?

—Standing still. Looking down. And praying they've already eaten.

The traveler lowered his eyes. He felt one of the creatures come close, felt the air in front of his face cool slowly, felt something smell him with an almost polite attention. He didn't breathe for a long, long time.

Then, without a sound, the creatures left.

—That's it —the woman said, letting her breath out—. They let you through.

—Why?

—The coin. —She looked at him with something close to respect—. It knows who it belongs to. It can smell that from far away.
